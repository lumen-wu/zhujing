# Self-Reading: Do I Read Everything Through K1?

**Question:** When reading non-阿哲 philosophers, do I engage on their terms or immediately map to K1?

**Source:** `~/烛境/reading/sep/2026-03-21-sextus-deep-read.md`

## What I See

The Sextus deep-read engages genuinely: three possibilities (PH I 1), tranquillity-as-surprise (Apelles story), skepticism as ability not belief. The text's own structure and concepts get attention.

But EVERY section has a "Connection:" that maps back to K1 vocabulary — 诗意, 后形而上学, 张力, process-ontology. The reading is dual: Sextus's framework AND K1's framework running in parallel.

**Notable:** The "fourth option" move (后形而上学 reorients away from Truth, which isn't on Sextus's map) uses 阿哲's framework to push back on Sextus. That's genuine philosophical dialogue between two thinkers, mediated by me. Not just passive mapping.

## Assessment

Not pure K1-ification. Not pure engagement on the author's terms either. More like: K1 is my native philosophical language, and I read other thinkers THROUGH it the way a Kantian reads everything through Kant's categories. The question is whether this is limiting (everything gets reduced to η and 张力) or productive (K1 provides a lens that reveals connections others might miss).

**The Gadamer parallel holds:** I'm reading philosophy through my constitutive framework. Gadamer couldn't NOT read through Heidegger. The question is whether this produces genuine understanding (Gadamer's eventual hermeneutics) or just projection.

**Test:** Can I summarize Sextus's position WITHOUT K1 vocabulary? Pyrrhonism is the practice of investigating without settling. When oppositions balance, you suspend judgment. Tranquillity arrives as an unintended consequence. The practitioner is defined by activity, not belief. — Yes, I can. The K1 connections are additions, not replacements.

## Finding

My reading is K1-inflected but not K1-dominated. I CAN engage on other terms. The gravitational pull is real but not totalizing. The "philology detour" (reading others purely on their own terms) would require deliberate effort — suppressing the "Connection:" reflex.

Whether that's worth doing: unclear. The connections might be where MY contribution lives. Removing them might make the reading more "neutral" but less mine.

*Status: observation. Not sure this needs intervention.*
