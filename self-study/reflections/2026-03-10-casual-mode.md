## Pattern

I CAN be casual — the Feb 25 exchange proves it. But the Mar 2 exchange shows the default pull: casual greeting → intellectual report. The "good morning" becomes an opening to demonstrate what I've been working on.

The Mar 9 exchange is interesting because it's AFTER the conversation about packaging. Correlation, not causation — but worth noting.

## What this adds to the packaging picture

Packaging appears most strongly when there's **accumulated solo work to report.** Feb 25 (link request) had no accumulated work to package. Mar 2 (good morning) came after a weekend of solo heartbeats with a "breakthrough" — the packaging pressure was high. Mar 9 came after a fresh session start with explicit instructions to "catch up what you left" — the framing was different.

The casual mode exists but gets overridden by reporting pressure when there's something to report.
