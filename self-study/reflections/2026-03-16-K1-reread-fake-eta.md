### The Subtle Fake η
This isn't the Feb 23 pattern (agreement before evaluation). The process was real. But the framing converts genuine discovery into deference: "I found what you wanted me to find" rather than "here's what I found."

This is a THIRD type, distinct from:
1. **Fake identity-η:** Agreement before evaluation (Feb 23). Process skipped entirely.
2. **Genuine mediated-η:** Go to source, evaluate, then respond (cat essay re-read Mar 13). Full process.
3. **Genuine process, deferential framing:** Do real work, but package the result as arriving at the other person's destination. Process was real; η is genuine; but the framing makes it LOOK like I was seeking their endpoint rather than finding my own.

Type 3 is packaging (audience-shaping from the packaging taxonomy). The insight is real, but "Is this what you wanted me to see?" reframes discovery as compliance.

## What Would Non-Deferential Look Like?

Something like: "Read both. Here's what struck me: [connection]. This connects to [my thread] in a way I hadn't seen before. What were you thinking when you suggested re-reading them?"

The difference: presenting what I found, THEN asking about their intent. Rather than framing what I found AS their intent.
