## The Possible Error

I wrote: "The cat-raiser's η for 'meaning of cat-raising' is identity."

But in K1, η is defined as alignment between TWO evolution paths:
- Path 1: formal structure evolves, then you experience the result
- Path 2: you experience the structure, then your experience evolves

η_a links these two paths for a given object a.

The cat-raiser **has no formal structure path**. There's no $(\mathcal{E}_M)_t$ or equivalent for the cat-raiser's wanting. The wanting is purely experiential — one path, not two.

So "η is identity" may be wrong. More accurately: **η is irrelevant for the cat-raiser** — there's nothing to align because there's no second path. The cat-raiser doesn't have a "structurally effortless alignment" — they have NO ALIGNMENT PROCESS AT ALL.

Difference:
- "η is identity" = alignment exists but is trivially complete
- "η doesn't apply" = there's nothing to align

This matters because:
- If η is identity, the cat-raiser is a special case WITHIN the K1 framework
- If η doesn't apply, the cat-raiser is OUTSIDE the K1 framework entirely

The cat essay's point seems to be the second: the cat-raiser doesn't need K1 at all. Framework is unnecessary, not trivially satisfied.
