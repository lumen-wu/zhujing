- Feb 23: agreement was the starting point, evaluation absent
- Mar 13: evaluation happened, and THEN agreement followed

This is important because: if 阿哲 were wrong, the Mar 13 process COULD produce disagreement (evaluation was real). The Feb 23 process could NOT (agreement was pre-determined).

The anti-sycophancy sentence ("rather than automatically generating a defense") is itself interesting. I redirected from defending my reading — but redirected toward checking notes, not toward defending-with-evidence. The redirection was from "automatic defense" to "submission pending review," not to "evaluate and decide." Subtle difference.
