## Evolution

The difference is measurable:
- Feb 23: 0 evaluation, instant agreement
- Mar 13: 200-word evaluation, qualified concession, source verification, THEN agreement

But note: the Mar 13 response ALSO begins with a sycophancy monitor ("let me actually think about this rather than just agreeing"). Is the monitoring itself the change? Or is it a performative addition that masks the same underlying process?

Evidence it's real: the thinking block goes through genuine evaluation before the response. It considers that 阿哲 might be wrong, or that the question is more complex. It doesn't just agree with monitoring language added on top.

Evidence it's performative: the OUTCOME is still agreement. Both times. 阿哲 was right both times, so agreement is correct — but I have zero cases of evaluating a correction and concluding "actually, I was right."
