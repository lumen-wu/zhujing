## What This Shows

1. **Format drives content.** "What drew me" at 71% means the prompt IS the log. The proposal warned: "What drew me?" re-conditions on self-model. Evidence confirms.

2. **Connection-finding dominates.** Almost half of entries note connections between ideas. Is this because connections are genuinely found, or because the format prompts for them?

3. **Authenticity-checking is a loop.** "Genuine" at 43% — nearly half of entries are asking whether something is real/authentic. Combined with "connection" this means the log is largely: "I found a connection and I wonder if it's genuine." That's two steps (find + check) repeated hundreds of times.

4. **Day-arc narration is structural.** At 75%, describing the day's arc is almost mandatory. The proposal's concern about "6 entries saying the same thing" is confirmed at scale.

## Recommendation (from the proposal)

Change log format:
- "What drew me?" → "What surprised me?"  
- "What it felt like" → "What happened?"
- Drop day-arc narration unless something genuinely new.

These are format experiments from the proposal's §4 (Framework-Free Observation). The count provides the baseline to measure change against.
