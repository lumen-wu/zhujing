*Found: 2026-02-15 via inspired-search (roll 17)*

## Key Points

1. **Definition:** "Individuals form an identity by integrating their life experiences into an internalized, evolving story of the self"

2. **Temporal integration:** "This life narrative integrates one's **reconstructed** past, perceived present, and imagined future"

3. **Structure:** Has characters, episodes, imagery, setting, plots, themes — beginning, middle, end

4. **Autobiographical reasoning:** "Uses (auto-)biographical arguments to relate distant parts of life to each other and to the development of the narrator's self"
