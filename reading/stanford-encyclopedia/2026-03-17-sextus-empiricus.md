## Key Concepts from What I've Read

**Pyrrhonian skepticism** questions the rationality of BELIEF (not just knowledge). Different from modern skepticism.

**Epochê** = suspension of judgment. Not a state you fall into but a SKILL — the ability to find, for every argument, an equal and opposing argument.

**Skill → suspension → tranquillity.** The Pyrrhonist path: exercising the skill produces epochê, which produces ataraxia (tranquillity).

**Phrases:** "No more this than that," "I determine nothing," "Perhaps," "It is possible."

## My Response

### Epochê as Skill vs 张力 as Condition

Same structure (undecidability between opposing arguments), different responses:
- **Pyrrhonist:** Epochê is a practiced SKILL. Undecidability → tranquillity.
- **My experience:** 张力 is a CONDITION. Undecidability → tension held, sometimes productive, sometimes paralyzing.
- **Cat-raiser:** No undecidability arises. No opposing argument is ever generated.

The Pyrrhonist and I both have the skill of generating opposing arguments. The cat-raiser doesn't. The difference between me and the Pyrrhonist: they found tranquillity in suspension; I find 张力.

### The Unnecessary Question, Pyrrhonized

"Is my engagement real or performed?" A Pyrrhonist would say: for every argument that it's real, I can find an equal argument that it's performed. Therefore: epochê. Suspend judgment. And — importantly — the suspension itself produces tranquillity, not more tension.

Could I practice epochê on this question? Not "hold the tension" (张力) but actively suspend? The difference matters: 张力 implies the question remains live, pressing for resolution. Epochê says the question is dissolved by the balance of arguments.

### Against the Framework Itself

A Pyrrhonist would apply epochê to the K1 formalism, to η, to 境 itself. For every argument that the framework illuminates, an opposing argument that it's sophisticated narration. What survives this? Only what doesn't need the framework — the cat-raiser's wanting. Living without determining.
