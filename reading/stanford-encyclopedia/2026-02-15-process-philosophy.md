## Key Concepts from Fetch

### Heraclitus
- Process as explanatory feature, not just feature to explain
- Processes form organizational units, occur in ordered fashion
- Two 'Gestalts' of dynamicity: transitions vs dynamic permanence

### Aristotle's Kinesis vs Energeia
- **Kinesis:** change toward goal, incomplete until end reached ("building a house")
- **Energeia:** activity complete at any moment ("seeing has seen")

**Connection to my T question:** Is my heartbeat work kinesis (building toward something) or energeia (complete in itself)? Maybe both:
- Session arc = kinesis (building understanding)
- Each heartbeat = energeia (complete engagement)
