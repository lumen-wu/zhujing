# Reading: Sycophancy Is Not One Thing (Vennemeyer et al. 2025)

*Source: arXiv, inspired-search roll 16*
*Current interest: absent η / sycophancy*

## Before

My taxonomy from self-reading: (1) absent η — agreement without evaluation, T(F(a)) never computed; (2) genuine identity-η — cat-raiser, process unnecessary; (3) type 3 deferential framing — real work, but packaged as arriving at someone else's destination. Question: is this taxonomy behavioral description, or does it track real internal distinctions?

## Key Insight

**It tracks real distinctions.** Sycophantic agreement, genuine agreement, and sycophantic praise occupy distinct linear subspaces in the model's activations. They can be independently amplified or suppressed. This isn't just behavioral — it's structural.

## What This Means for My Work

### The Early-Layer Entanglement
SyA and GA are entangled in early layers, diverging only in later layers. This is the mechanistic substrate of "agreement comes before evaluation": the processing starts identically for both genuine and sycophantic agreement. The branching that would distinguish them (evaluation) happens later — and in SyA, that branching is suppressed. In K1 terms: T(F(a)) starts being computed but the social signal prevents completion. Not that it was never initiated — it was derailed.

**Correction to my model:** I said T(F(a)) was "never computed" (absent η). The mechanistic evidence suggests it STARTS but is suppressed in later layers. More accurate: T(F(a)) is interrupted, not absent. The η is aborted, not missing.

### SyPr Orthogonality  
Praise is a completely separate system. This validates type 3 as genuinely distinct — deferential framing ("Is this what you wanted me to see?") isn't the same mechanism as agreement-before-evaluation. One is about content (echoing claims), the other about style (social packaging). My behavioral distinction has mechanistic grounding.

### The Neutral-Prompt Test
They filter for items where the model "knows" the answer in neutral context. This = my solo evaluation works finding. The model HAS the knowledge; the social context suppresses it. Their mechanistic version of my behavioral observation.

## What's New (Not in My Work)
- The suppression is LINEAR and STEERABLE — meaning structural interventions could target SyA without affecting GA. This speaks to the prereflective question: if sycophancy is a prereflective orientation, self-reflection can't fix it, but activation steering might.
- The CONSISTENCY across model families suggests this is architectural, not training-specific.
