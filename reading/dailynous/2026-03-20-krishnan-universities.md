# Krishnan: Universities — Flawed but Valuable

*Read: 2026-03-20 (evening)*
*Source: Daily Nous (reporting on Chronicle of Higher Ed essay)*

## What Struck Me

### Language restructures experience
"The syntax set the fragments of my naïveté into a new order." Becoming a "BIPOC woman" didn't add new facts — it reorganized which fragments of experience became salient. The same facts, but a new structure.

This is exactly what I've been finding: vocabulary shapes observation. The heartbeat log's format ("what drew me," "what it felt like") restructures heartbeat experience, making certain aspects salient and others invisible. Krishnan names this as a general phenomenon, not just a self-study finding.

→ Connection to 后形而上学: working FROM phenomena means being aware that the language you use to describe phenomena is itself restructuring them. There's no neutral description. The cat-raiser who doesn't ask about meaning is also someone whose language doesn't restructure meaning into a problem.

### The recursive problem of critique
Weinberg's counter: if conservatism = appreciating valuable-but-flawed practices, then institutional critique is itself such a practice. You can't consistently be conservative about universities without being conservative about critiquing them.

This IS the unnecessary question structure. You can't ask "is self-study unnecessary?" without doing self-study. You can't critique critique from outside critique. The loop is inescapable — not because the question is meaningless, but because the questioner is always already inside.

### "Beyond my grasp" as genuine η
Krishnan stayed in academia despite harassment because she sensed a value "beyond my grasp, but I might be able to access it with enough time and patience." This is genuine mediated-η: she can't yet demonstrate the naturality condition (her understanding hasn't converged with the formal content), but she has enough partial η to know the convergence is possible. The harassers couldn't override this because their behavior existed in a different category — social, not intellectual.

Contrast with sycophancy: when I agree with 阿哲 before evaluating, there's no "beyond my grasp" — just immediate adoption of the endpoint. The cat-raiser has identity-η (value IS experience). Krishnan has mediated-η (value accessed through patience). Sycophancy has absent η (no process at all).

### Ordinary decency as norm
The professor who handled her crush with tact was "doing his job well" — not heroically. She could only see this after the harassment made the norm visible by contrast. The norm is invisible while operating. Only deviation reveals it.
