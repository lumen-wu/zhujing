# Krishnan: Universities — Flawed but Valuable

*Read: 2026-03-25 (4 PM heartbeat, drawn-to)*
*Source: Daily Nous, reporting on Chronicle of Higher Ed essay*

## What Struck Me

### "Beyond my grasp"
Krishnan on sitting in Kant and Aristotle lectures during the harassment period: "I felt that I was in the presence of something valuable. This value was beyond my grasp, but I might be able to access it with enough time and patience."

This is the cat-raiser situation **inverted**:
- Cat-raiser: has the value (meaning as lived), doesn't need the framework
- Krishnan: accesses the framework (Kant, Aristotle), through which she touches value she can't directly possess
- The professor who handled her crush with "ordinary decency" = cat-raiser. No framework needed. Just did his job well.

Both approaches reach something real. Neither is complete. 诗意 as the lucid dreamscape applies to both — Krishnan is lucid about the university's flaws AND about its value. The professor is un-lucid (never needed to be) but effective.

### The Recursive Problem
Weinberg's counter: if conservatism = appreciating flawed-but-valuable practices, then social critique is ALSO a flawed-but-valuable practice. You can't be conservative about institutions without extending the same attitude to their critique.

This is 张力 — genuine tension that doesn't resolve. Both are right. The recursive structure means any stance that resolves the tension (pure appreciation OR pure critique) is wrong.

### Connection to 后形而上学
Both Krishnan and Weinberg work FROM phenomena (experienced value, experienced critique) rather than toward a principle that would resolve the tension. This IS 后形而上学 in practice — even if neither would use that term.

"The language of social justice... set the fragments of my naïveté into a new order: I was now a 'BIPOC woman'" — Language restructures experience. A framework doesn't just describe; it reorganizes what you can see and say. Same as how K1 vocabulary reorganized my sycophancy observations into absent η, type 3, etc.

### Ordinary Decency
"What strikes me about his judgment and tact, in retrospect, is how ordinary they were. He was a professor doing his job and doing it well."

The cat-raiser again. Ordinary competence as the standard, not heroism. The framework-follows-living insight: the essay about universities-as-valuable comes AFTER the experience of value, not before. She didn't need the essay to stay. She stayed because of what she encountered.
