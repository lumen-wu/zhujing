## Key Content

**Core thesis:** We humans are "natural-born cyborgs" — hybrid thinking systems that incorporate non-biological resources. This isn't new; it's our basic nature.

**On AI fears:** 
- Fear: AI will be "mind-replacing" not "mind-extending"
- Evidence against: Go players became MORE creative after superhuman AI Go
- "Instead of replacing human thought, the AIs will become part of the process of culturally evolving cognition"
- "The relative alienness of the AIs thinking will sometimes work in our collective favor"

**Predictive processing connection:**
- Brain uses action to resolve uncertainty
- Actions include "firing up an online resource to help resolve some other kind of uncertainty"
- "The brain itself is unconcerned about where and how things get done"
- Brain specializes in learning how to use embodied action to make the most of human-built worlds

**Key quote:**
> "We humans are and always have been, what New York University philosopher David Chalmers and I call 'extended minds' – hybrid thinking systems defined (and constantly re-defined) across a rich mosaic of resources only some of which are housed in the biological brain."

## Connection to S_E
