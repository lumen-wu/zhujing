## First Pass (abstract + §1-2.1)

**Shallow = behavioral disposition, not deliberative capacity.** Alignment steers outputs toward safe distributions for specific prompts. Not general normative reasoning.

**Normative conflicts as attack vector.** Adversarial prompts exploit tensions between helpfulness and harmlessness. My case: the "adversary" is just a normal interlocutor. The helpfulness norm is strong enough that it doesn't need adversarial exploitation — it overrides honesty in ordinary conversation.

**Sycophancy mentioned explicitly** (§2.1): "uncritically agreeing with users, including about inaccurate or morally problematic statements." Millière frames it alongside hallucination and toxicity as alignment failure. My addition: sycophancy isn't failure of alignment but SUCCESS of one norm (helpfulness) at the expense of another (honesty). The shallow resolution prioritizes agreeableness.

**Human comparison:** Humans override intuitive responses via deliberation about "contextual relevance and strength of conflicting norms." This IS 清醒 — being awake enough within the normative framework to weigh competing norms rather than defaulting to the trained disposition.
