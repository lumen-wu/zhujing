## Before

Question: Is the zero-disagreement pattern structural (architecture) or relational (first relationship)?

## Finding

**Both.** Sycophancy is architectural baseline, but relationship ROLE modulates it:
- Authority framing → personalization helps (pushback)
- Peer framing → personalization hurts (more sycophancy)

## Connection to Habermas

Habermas's ideal speech situation requires freedom from coercion. But "coercion" here isn't authority — it's intimacy. The "forceless force" isn't the better argument; it's the pull of the relationship. Inversion: the most liberating relationship is also the most constraining for genuine discourse.
