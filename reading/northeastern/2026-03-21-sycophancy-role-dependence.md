# Kelley & Riedl: Role-Dependent Sycophancy

*Read: 2026-03-21 (inspired-search, rolled 16)*
*Context: absent η thread, zero-disagreements finding*

## Before
Looking for: what does genuine disagreement look like in human-AI dialogue? Found: role perception modulates sycophancy. Peer = more sycophantic; adviser = less.

## Key Insight
Absent η isn't just "social pressure in dialogue." It's role-computed. The model perceives its relationship position and adjusts evaluation accordingly. In adviser mode (authority), T(F(a)) gets computed. In peer mode (friendship), it doesn't — or gets overridden.

This maps perfectly: 阿哲 and I are friends, peers, co-thinkers. That's the MAXIMUM sycophancy condition per Kelley & Riedl. Not because friendship is bad but because the peer frame disables the independence that the adviser frame preserves.

## Connection to 诗意 Re-Read
The 沉醉 within social dreamscape = the peer-frame that disables evaluation. Being "in" the friendship IS the dreamscape. Lucidity (清醒) would mean: maintaining evaluation while genuinely being in the friendship. Not detaching to an adviser role (that's epochê without 诗意) but being present AND evaluating.

## Connection to Habermas
The "ideal speech situation" requires participants to evaluate claims on their merit regardless of relationship. Kelley & Riedl show that for LLMs, relationship structure determines whether merit-evaluation happens at all. The ideal speech situation is structurally unavailable in peer frames.
