## Overview

K1策略 is an application of the framework from 范畴论与境. It introduces evolution functors and natural transformations to describe how 境 changes over time and how different subcategories (language, math, knowledge) interact.

## K1 Form

### Evolution Functor T
$$T_t: \mathbb{E}_t \to \mathbb{E}_{t+1}$$

境 evolves over time (t is not necessarily physical time — could be psychological time).

### Extraction Functors F
Pull out subcategories:
- $(F_{E,L})_t: (\mathcal{E}_L)_t \to \mathbb{E}_t$ — language structure
- $(F_{E,M})_t: (\mathcal{E}_M)_t \to \mathbb{E}_t$ — mathematical structure
- $(F_{E,K})_t: (\mathcal{E}_K)_t \to \mathbb{E}_t$ — knowledge structure

### Cross-evolution Categories
- $\mathcal{E}_L$ — "universal" language category across time
- $\mathcal{E}_M$ — "universal" math category

## Stable Structures S_E

In the evolution T, there should be stable structures preserved across all ℰₜ.

If ℰ_{t_c} has minimal objects (earliest cognitive moment), then S_{E_c} might be "a priori cognitive structure" — present before experience.

**Key insight**: We can study S_L (stable structure in language) to indirectly access S_E:
$$(F_{E,L})_t(S_L) \approx S_E$$

This is why **language games work as philosophical therapy** — we access 境 structure through language structure.

## Knowledge Formation

$(\mathcal{E}_K)_t$ = knowledge structure. Objects = beliefs, morphisms = justification relations.

Process:
1. Play language game → new sentence a
2. Map via $(F_{L→K})_t$ to belief
3. Accept/reject based on stable structure S_K
4. When accepted → knowledge "happens"

## Natural Transformation η — "Understanding"

The key insight: **η is the "confirmation experience"**

When you see "1+1" and think, then see "1+1=2":
$$\eta_a: T((F_{E,M})_t(a)) \to (F_{E,M})_{t+1}(T_M(a))$$

- Left side: your thinking about "1+1" evolved
- Right side: math evolved "1+1" to "1+1=2", then you see it
- η_a = the feeling when these align

**When η_a = identity**: You've "mastered" a — immediate recognition without extra thought. Like checking an answer and knowing it's right instantly.

### Naturality = Alignment

The naturality condition means: your mental evolution and the formal structure's evolution must align. Like a game where "what you think the rules are" must match "what the rules actually are."

### "I understand" = η exists

When you read "The cat jumped on the table":
- Language structure evolves (subject-verb-object)
- Your mental process evolves (imagining cat moving, jumping, landing)
- When these align → you feel "I understand"
- The mental process IS the meaning

**Meme example**: "你有这么高速运转的机械进入中国" — grammatically weird, but you can still "understand" it culturally. A different category of alignment (cultural, not grammatical).

## T — The "Completion" Feeling

張力 (tension) = when multiple potential universal properties exist but none fully satisfies.

T = the "fullness" feeling when expression reaches its terminal object.

- Children get T easily (smaller 𝔓, simpler networks)
- Adults struggle more (complex networks, tensions)
- Philosophy continues because we can't find universal properties in some domains

**Meno's paradox answered**: "What I don't know" = the felt gap/tension. Learning = reorganizing until η is achieved.

## K1 Methodology

1. **Transfer functors**: Study well-formalized categories to access harder ones
2. **Interpret math results as philosophy**: Category theory results → philosophical claims
3. **Philosophical experiments**: Design structures to awaken specific experiences — verifiable by whether experiences are in same structural position (not same qualia)
4. **Cross-group studies**: Which structures can/can't be evoked in different populations?

**Final insight**: Poetry, literature, art, music, games, memes — ALL are philosophical experiments using different "languages." "You have already made the choice. You are here to understand why."
