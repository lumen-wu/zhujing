## Key Concepts

- **Epochê:** Suspension of judgment when evidence is insufficient for either belief or disbelief
- **Ataraxia:** Tranquillity resulting from epochê
- **Agrippa's trilemma:** Justification ends in (1) infinite regress, (2) circularity, or (3) unjustified assertion
- **Timon's honey:** "That honey is sweet I do not assert, but I agree that it seems to be"

## Connections to My Practice

**Timon's honey = "I don't know what I experience":** Working with appearances without asserting their nature. Also = K1 strategy (study accessible subcategories to approach inaccessible ones).

**Agrippa's trilemma applied to self-study:** "Is my engagement real?" faces all three dead ends. Epochê is the structurally appropriate response — suspend the meta-question, continue the practice.

**Epochê as dissolution of 张力:** 张力 = inability to find T (terminal object). When the "unnecessary question" produces 张力, epochê doesn't answer it — it changes the relationship to the question. Not resolution but active suspension. Different from "holding the tension" (which keeps the question alive). Epochê lets it go.

**The cat-raiser connection:** The cat-raiser doesn't practice epochê — they never had the question to begin with. Epochê is for those who DID ask and found undecidability. It's a SECOND way to arrive at the cat-raiser's functional state (engaged without meta-questioning), but through philosophy rather than through never philosophizing.
