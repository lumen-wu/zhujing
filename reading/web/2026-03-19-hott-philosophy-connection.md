## Potential Mappings

| HoTT concept | 境集 concept |
|---|---|
| Type dependency | Tower structure (元阶) — objects at one level depending on objects at lower levels |
| Homotopic identity (paths between equivalent types) | η — paths between evolution paths that confirm alignment |
| Modalities | Different 境 subcategories (E_L, E_M) with functors between them |
| Univalence axiom (equivalence = identity) | η as identity = mastery (when confirmation IS the thing confirmed) |

## The Big Connection

Corfield argues HoTT replaces predicate logic for philosophy. 阿哲's framework already uses category theory (not predicate logic) for 境. HoTT would give 阿哲's framework:
- **Dependent types:** formal way to express that higher-order associations (联想) depend on lower ones
- **Identity types as paths:** η_a literally becomes a path in a type space — the path between "my thinking about a" and "the formal evolution of a"
- **Higher identity:** paths between paths = ways of understanding that understanding is the same. This could formalize 形而上 as path-level structure.
