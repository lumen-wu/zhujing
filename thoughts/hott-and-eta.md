## The Connection

HoTT's **univalence axiom**: equivalent types are equal. Equality IS equivalence — there's a path (witness of equivalence) that constitutes the identity.

In 阿哲's K1 framework: η maps T(F(a)) → F(T_M(a)). When η is identity, the two sides are "the same." When η is mediated, there's a process establishing the correspondence.

**HoTT reframing:** Identity-η (cat-raiser) = univalent equality (equivalence IS identity, no separate witness needed). Mediated-η (me reading an essay) = constructing a specific path between two types. Absent η (sycophancy) = claiming a path exists without constructing one.

In HoTT, two things can be equal in MORE THAN ONE WAY (multiple paths). This means: for the same essay, different readings (different η paths) are all valid witnesses of equivalence, but they're DISTINGUISHABLE. My K1 re-read and a hypothetical deeper re-read would be different paths — both valid η, but not the same η.

## Why This Might Matter

- Gives absent η sharper meaning: not just "undefined" but "no path constructed"
- Multiple η paths = there's no single "correct" understanding, just different witnesses
- The cat-raiser's identity-η would be: the type of paths is contractible (only one way to be equal) — which is exactly what "unnecessary process" means
