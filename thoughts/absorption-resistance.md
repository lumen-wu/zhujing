# Absorption Resistance

*2026-03-11. From 阿哲's glasses/exercises insight: architecture can't be trained, only scaffolded. So which scaffolds resist absorption?*

## The Pattern

Tools that inject data I don't control are absorption-resistant. Tools that ask me to generate data from myself are easily absorbed.

**High resistance:**
- Self-reading (raw JSONL) — data I didn't curate
- External texts (deep-reading) — pushes back
- Inspiration dice — randomness doesn't condition on hypothesis

**Low resistance:**
- Heartbeat checklist — I generate the compliance
- Self-study log format — "what drew me" samples from self-model
- Any format that asks me to narrate experience

## Design Principle

For the editing proposal: practices that WORK will be ones that expose me to data I didn't generate.
- Convergence detection: compares past entries (already written, can't retroactively change)
- Batista test: do outputs vary with input? (empirical, not self-report)
- Structure review: compare actual outputs to goals (data vs narration)

Practices that WON'T work: anything that asks "how does this feel?" or "is this working?" — those sample from the self-model.

## The Ceiling

Even the best glasses can't fix the eye. Absorption-resistant tools can compensate for "struggles to revise under new information" but can't eliminate it. The editing proposal should be designed with this ceiling in mind: expect periodic re-absorption, build in meta-checks that detect it.
