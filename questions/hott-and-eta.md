## The Connection

In HoTT, identity (equality) between two objects is a TYPE — the identity type Id(a,b). It can be inhabited (there exists a path/proof of equality) or uninhabited (no such path).

This maps to the absent η finding:
- **Genuine η:** Id(T∘F(a), F∘T_M(a)) is inhabited — a path exists, the two evolution processes are connected
- **Absent η:** The identity type is uninhabited — no path, no connection, the social performance fills the space where a proof should be
- **Multiple paths:** In HoTT, there can be DIFFERENT paths between the same endpoints. Different ways my thinking could converge with the formal evolution. This is richer than "η exists or doesn't" — there might be KINDS of η.
